# Verbatim from the Solarpunk Game Doc (opening radio message).
En-Joy Transmission — Danu
Comms, En-Joy

<i>A woman’s voice warbles through unstable sound frequencies emitting from the vintage radio:</i>

Our lives are supported by the hands of our ancestors. Their hands were capable of rewilding earth, wiping tears away, cradling wounded hearts, animal and human alike. They held on even when their grip weakened and splinters broke skin. Now, we find ourselves nestled safely in their palms. For them, our gratitude runs deep and spreads into the soil of our home.

As members of En-Joy, we also offer our palms, so that future generations can rest. We repair what is good, dismantle what is not, and plant what grows. Today, we send our comrade, Escropita, to the Xanthe Terra region on Mars, the home of the Mycari, who have requested contact. This is the first time a human has walked on Mars in nearly 100 years. Our legacy on Mars is the spectacular failure of the Joy Tech initiative, whose members did not bother to learn the mycari’s way when they tried to dominate the scarlet planet. Escropita, and her companion Daffodil, are making the first steps to change that. May you be safe as you repair what is good, dismantle what is not, and plant what grows on the grave of human hubris. For you, our gratitude runs deep and spreads into the soil of our home
