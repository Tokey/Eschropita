# Verbatim from the Solarpunk Game Doc (Lore object 1). Line 1 = title, line 2 = author, then the text.
Aniya Freeman — Log 240
Chief Engineer, JoyTech Xanthe Terra

Well, surprise to no one! Life support system optimization, that little thing keeping us all literally breathing, has to wait because Milton Hargrave wants the resources dedicated to the stupid “data obelisk.” It’s actually insane that he scolded me about this. Anyone with even a little appreciation for science would consider my work a marvel. I was able to control for gravitational differences, atmosphere, radiation, AND temperature. But no. That means nothing if I cannot somehow generate enough power for this ridiculous thing.

My nerves are fried. Would my family be disappointed if I came back home? I’ve never been able to send this kind of money to Cité Soleil before this gig. I wonder how manman’s treatment is going. It’s not like I can ask. I haven’t been successful making comms tech. Not like I have any support, time, or energy for that, anyway. Maybe it’s better I am not talking to them. I don’t know how I would explain to manman what I've been doing. I know she’d give me that look. Her eyes can make me feel so small when I know better. I sometimes see the Mycari, and something in the way they look…it makes me feel small, too. I know whatever Hargrave wants will be bad for them. …I can accept my isolation here as punishment. I’ll eat the shame if it means she can stay in treatment. I can work for a maniac so long as manman can stay at the good hospital. With good doctors. No surprise bills. I don’t need to be okay with myself. I can do this as long as I just keep my head down. Even away from the mirror.
