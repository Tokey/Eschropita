# Verbatim from the Solarpunk Game Doc (Lore object 2, found when the killswitch is turned off).
Vera Sterling — Unsent Message
Biologist, JoyTech Xanthe Terra

I had no idea when I stepped on the shuttle that day that I would be here, betraying my boss and my personal hero.

The opportunity to work under Aniyah Freeman to create the first ever shared space between humans and alien life in recorded history. Even now, I feel overcome by the glorious possibility of it all. I was a baby grad student with one theoretical paper on subsurface microbial life on Mars, before I was approached. It was like being plucked by the hands of a god.

I cannot say I regret my time here. Witnessing Mycari up close, watching the way they communicate, observing their symbiotic relationality with fungi. The study of life is forever changed, and I feel honored to have spent any of my time at this event horizon.

What I regret is my naivete. Of course it wasn’t about creating new knowledge. No one pays for that. When Hargrave asked me to collect their biodata, I cannot say I did not want it. But, this surveillance gets more invasive by the day. They are not animals; the more I interact with them, the more I see their intelligence and curiosity. I don’t know how long I can keep building faulty tracking probes before he just hires another tech-worshipping fool to do it.

And Aniyah. The heartbreak of my life. She didn’t even flinch when I told her. The woman whose footsteps I followed into space, just doing whatever he wants. And the insanity-inducing truth is that she’s talented enough to do it all. She runs herself ragged for him.

For the data obelisk project, I offered to build the external shields. She was eager to have help. My sabotage was simple to pull off, really; just some modular structures that can be jostled by a tornado. They clip perfectly to the windmill, so that poor fools don’t die here from power outages.

A well-rested mechanical engineer could easily identify this structural issue, so I was sweating with anxiety when I tested the shard integrity in front of Aniyah and Hargrave. Thankfully, she was too exhausted to notice, and Hargrave is a scientifically illiterate moron who lacks the skills to comprehend the forces he toys with.

It’s a pathetic sabotage, really. I’m still just some useful idiot who was all to eager to come to mars and help a neo colonizer. But I can’t stand by and do nothing. Not now that I know.

I cannot conspire with the tornados to hit the obelisk, but maybe it will make this whole cursed project harder to accomplish.

I feel afraid, and I want to delete this in case I am found out. But I can’t be naive and a coward and sleep with a conscience, now can I?
