# Level textures

All textures in `Assets/Overhaul/Resources/Overhaul/Textures/` are **CC0** (public domain) from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com),
downloaded at 1K and repacked for HDRP: `_Albedo` (sRGB), `_Normal` (OpenGL / Y+), `_Mask` (HDRP mask map: R metallic,
G ambient occlusion, B detail 0, A smoothness = 1 - roughness, 512 px).
